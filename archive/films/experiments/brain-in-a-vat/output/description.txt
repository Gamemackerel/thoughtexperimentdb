What if your brain had been removed from your body, kept alive in a vat of nutrients, and wired to a computer that feeds it a perfect simulation of the world? Everything would feel exactly as it does now. So how could you ever know that it isn't happening?

This short film traces the idea from René Descartes' evil demon (1641) to Hilary Putnam's brain in a vat (1981), sets out the skeptic's argument that threatens almost everything we believe, and walks through Putnam's surprising reply: that if we were brains in vats, we couldn't even truly think so. It ends with the objections that keep the debate alive.
