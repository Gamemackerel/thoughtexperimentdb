A runaway trolley is heading for five people. You can pull a lever to send it onto a side track, where it will kill one person instead. Should you?

Philippa Foot introduced the case in 1967, and Judith Jarvis Thomson turned it into one of the most famous puzzles in ethics: why does diverting the trolley feel permissible when so many other ways of killing one to save five do not? This short film walks through Foot's original driver case, Thomson's bystander version and the distinction between doing and allowing harm, and ends with Thomson's surprising change of mind in 2008.
