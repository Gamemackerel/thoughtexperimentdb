# bugs · room 1 (house, trolley-problem, brain-in-a-vat, platos-cave, ship-of-theseus)

## Summary
- No crashes, no console errors and no soft-locks in about 1,000 s of play. Every listed ending in room 1 is reachable (trolley 2/2, vat 2/2, cave 3/3, Theseus 2/2), Play again resets level state cleanly, and `ted.done`/`ted.journal` save correctly.
- The worst problem is a pattern: **three endings sit one E-press from where you stand** (the vat bench at spawn, the cave's "Sit back down" the instant the chains fall, and the house portal you respawn inside). Mashing E or talking to a neighbour ends a vignette before it has started.
- **Speech bubbles get stuck on screen** (all vignettes, in `main.js`): if you talk to someone twice before their bubble expires, the first bubble freezes half-faded in place until you leave the level.
- **The trolley ignores you on the rails.** You can stand on either track and it passes straight through you, which undercuts the vignette's own last line.
- Queued narration isn't cancelled when the state changes: "Should you pull it?" plays after you've pulled, the cave's "choose" line plays over the game-over card, "too bright" plays outside, and the vat's key question gets cut when you step off quickly.

## Findings
### house
- **[minor] [input]** You come back from a vignette standing inside that portal's trigger radius (e.g. back from the cave at (-5.6, 3.4), 1.4 m from the door, radius 2.3; back from the book at (-2.6, 2.8)). One E/Space (a natural "skip" press as the card closes) sends you straight back in. Recipe: game-over card → Esc (confirm) → `press e` → back in brain-in-a-vat 2 s later (t=967→969.8).
  Suggestion: spawn returning players ~1 m outside the radius, or ignore interact input for ~0.8 s after a level loads.
- **[polish] [clarity]** Two portals share the prompt "Open the door" (purple door → cave, sky door → Theseus; `debug` #3 and #5). Between them, near the ladder, you can't tell from the prompt which one you'll get. Suggestion: "Open the purple door" / "Open the sky door" (or name the destination once it's been visited).
- **[polish] [ui]** Esc on a game-over card opens the "Leave this vignette and return to the house?" confirm, though the vignette is already over. Suggestion: on the card, Esc should act as "Back to the house" with no confirm (or do nothing).
### trolley-problem
- **[major] [bug/collision]** You can stand on either track and the trolley drives straight through you. Recipe: run 1 pull the lever, `walk 12 -6` (next to the lone worker on the side track) and stay there; or on run 2 `walk 8 0` (in front of the five on the main track). The trolley passes through the teal figure while the workers beside you are knocked flying; you are untouched and no line reacts. The ending even says "someone is standing on the track", and you literally were. It also offers a free, unacknowledged "stand in the way" option that the game pretends not to see.
  Evidence: shots/009-t157.jpg (trolley overlapping player on the side track), shots/012-t211.jpg (on the main track, five knocked, player standing inside the dust). `blockers()` only keeps you out of the trolley while `!player.locked`, and nothing checks the player's position when the trolley commits.
  Suggestion: either make the rails non-walkable during `slow`/`go` (push the player off the ballast with a line like "You step back off the rails."), or treat standing on the committed route as the self ending (knock the player flying like the others, then `self_end`). The second is closer to the fat-man/self-sacrifice variants and would be a nice discovery.
- **[minor] [narrative/bug]** "Should you pull it?" fires on a timer (trolley x > -20) even when you already pulled the lever. Recipe: at `slow`, `use Pull the lever` straight away: "This lever switches the tracks." → a few seconds later "Five people…", "On the side track…", "Should you pull it?" while the lever is already pulled (t=122–134). And when you never walk near the lever (run 1, stood at spawn), "it" has no referent because the `lever` line only fires in the lever trigger.
  Evidence: trolley-problem.js ~line 260 (`voice.say('ask')` guarded only by `voice.said.has('ask')` and `!S.twist`).
  Suggestion: skip `ask` if `S.route !== 'main'` (or say a variant like "Will you leave it there?"); if `lever` hasn't been said yet, say it (or "Should you pull the lever?") before `ask`.
- **[minor] [save/journal]** The journal keeps only the latest ending and re-stamps its date. Recipe: finish with "You chose yourself", Play again, finish with "Someone is always on the track": `ted.journal` now only has the second ending; the first is gone. Editing the answer in the journal also rewrites `at` to the edit time (19:58:21 → 19:58:45).
  Evidence: localStorage `ted.journal` before/after.
  Suggestion: store `endings: [..]` (set of titles reached, with the date first reached) and show them all ("2 of 2 endings"); keep `at` as the ending date and add `editedAt` if needed.
- **[polish] [state]** After the self ending `S.runs` stays at the previous value (1), so any code or analytics reading `runs` miss the final run. Cheap to fix in `afterSelf` (`S.runs++`, push `'self'` to `S.choices`).
- **[minor] [flow]** In run 1 there is no in-world way home (the frame and "Step back through the frame" only exist after the twist), only Esc. A player who wants to leave before choosing has to know about Esc. Evidence: `debug` at t=15.7 shows `Step back through the frame [disabled]`, frameOn 0.
  Suggestion: show the frame (not the prompt text) from the start behind the spawn so the way out is visible, or accept this and mention Esc on the arrival toast.
### brain-in-a-vat
- **[major] [pacing/ending]** The "You stayed" ending can be triggered 3 seconds after arriving, by one E/Space press at the spawn. Recipe: Play again (or enter via the book) → `use Sit down` at t≈479.4 (the bench is 3.5 m from the spawn and its prompt is live from frame one). The vignette ends before the player has seen the flickering edge or heard "If none of this were real, how would you ever know?"; the queued "The grass… feels solid" line then plays over the sitting. The ending card says "You never found out whether the sun was real", but the player never even had the question.
  Evidence: phase `explore` → `over` at 479.4, 0.9 s after "A sunny afternoon." `enabled: () => S.phase === 'explore' && S.layer === 0` (brain-in-a-vat.js:100).
  Suggestion: enable "Sit down" only after the edge has been seen (`S.edgeSeen`) or after the `ask` line has played, or let the first sit be a non-ending rest ("You sit for a while.") and only end on sitting after the doubt has been raised. Also move the bench a few metres away from the spawn so a stray Space doesn't end the scene.
- **[minor] [narrative]** "The world ends here. Just past the edge, it flickers." is skipped if you reach the edge and step off quickly (third play: walked straight to the edge, `Step off the edge` at t=500.0; only "You step off the edge." fired). `edgeSeen` is set but `voice.stop()` in onUse kills the queued `edge`/`ask` lines, so the central question "If none of this were real…" is never heard on that run.
  Suggestion: in the `Step off the edge` onUse, if `ask` hasn't been said yet, let `edge` finish (or say `ask` after landing in the lab) instead of `voice.stop()`.
- **[polish] [state]** "Look at the machine" is one-shot across layers: its two lines are used up in the lab of layer 1, so in layer 2 the machine (a new machine) has no prompt (debug at t=464: `Look at the machine [disabled]`). Layers 1 and 2 are otherwise identical, and layer 2 has nothing to do but walk to the edge. Suggestion: give layer 2's machine one different line (e.g. that it has the same reels) or leave the prompt available with the last line repeating.
### platos-cave
- **[major] [bug/input]** Talking to your neighbour while chained ends the vignette by accident. Recipe: at `chained`, `hold d 1` (face the prisoner on the right), then press E every ~3 s to hear "Best seat in the house, this." / "What's behind us? Nothing. Just rock.". The chains loosen at t=660.2 and the prompt under you changes from "Talk" to "Sit back down" (radius 2, you're standing on SEAT). The next E (t=663.1) plays "You turn back to the wall…" and the game-over card "You kept watching" appears. You never stood up or turned round. Same with no talking: a single E right after "Your chains have come loose." ends it (t=633.9 → over).
  Evidence: second and third cave plays; platos-cave.js:151 (`Sit back down` enabled as soon as `phase === 'free'`).
  Suggestion: enable "Sit back down" only once the player has moved away from the seat (e.g. >2.5 m, or after the `turn` line), and give it priority below "Talk" at the seat. This is the same "ending one E away from the spawn" pattern as the bench in Brain in a Vat.
- **[major] [bug/narrative]** Ending under the tree leaves the arrival lines running over the ending. Recipe: walk out fast (`walk 0 5`, `walk 15 6`), then at once `use Sit under the tree`. Captions in order: "A path leads up, towards a light." (already outside), "It's too bright to look at." (outside, after the transit), "Outside. The real things…", "You stay in the light.", then **"You could stay up here. Or go back down, and tell them."** after the ending line, on screen together with the game-over card "You stayed in the light" (t=711.5–712.7).
  Evidence: platos-cave.js:215: the anonymous async `outside`→`choose` sequence is not cancelled when `ending()` runs; `ending()` also queues its line behind `outside`.
  Suggestion: in `ending()` call `voice.stop()` first, and guard the transit coroutine (`if (S.phase !== 'free') return;` before `say('choose')`). Drop queued `climb`/`bright` lines once `S.where === 'out'` (e.g. say them with a "stale if the area changed" flag).
- **[minor] [flow]** After "Go back down", the cave mouth no longer leads out (the mouth transition requires `!S.returned`), and "Sit back down" is disabled too. Walking to the mouth (`walk 18 6`, `hold w 3`) just faces a dark wall (shots/040-t596.jpg). The only options are "Tell them" or Esc. That's defensible (going back is a commitment), but nothing tells the player, and they can't change their mind.
  Suggestion: either allow going back up (and say "You climb out again." then let them sit under the tree) or have one line on return saying there's no going back up ("Behind you, the way up has gone dark.").
- **[polish] [flow]** "Go back down" only appears after the `choose` line (`S.saidChoose`). A player who turns straight round to the arch after arriving outside gets no prompt for ~6 s. Suggestion: enable it from arrival outside; the line can still come.
### ship-of-theseus
- **[major] [bug/ui] (global, main.js)** Speech bubbles freeze on screen. Recipe: at `choose`, walk to the plank keeper (12.5, -0.2), press E ("I kept every one. Seemed a shame to burn them."), press E again within ~3 s ("Good wood, this. Just old."). The first bubble never fades: it stays at about half opacity, pinned where it was in screen space, while you walk to the fisherman, board the ship and sail away. It's still floating over the sea on the game-over card (shots/047-bubble.jpg, shots/049-t868.jpg, t=834→871). Cause: `ctx.speak` replaces the speaker's entry in `bubbles` with a new `key`, so the old `ui.label('speech-'+oldKey)` is never updated or hidden again. It affects every `talk()` in the game (trolley workers, cave prisoners…).
  Suggestion: in `speak()`, if `bubbles` already has that target, hide its label first (`ui.label('speech-'+old.key, 0, …)`) or reuse the old key; also hide on delete (`if (b.t > b.secs) { ui.label(…, 0 …); bubbles.delete(k); }`).
- **[minor] [input] (global)** With the notebook open, the game keeps running and still takes input underneath: W moves you (6.3 → 8.0 in 1 s) and E triggers the prompt you can't see ("Replace an old plank" fired with the notebook covering the screen, t=882.2). In the trolley that means pulling the lever blind, or the trolley passing the junction while you read.
  Suggestion: while the notebook is open, disable player movement and interaction (like the journal does, `player.enabled = false`), and ideally pause `level.update` (the `busy` flag) so reading costs nothing.
- **[polish] [clarity]** The two boarding prompts are 1.8 m apart on the pier centre line ((16, -0.9) new, (16, 0.9) old), while the ships sit on opposite sides of the pier. Which prompt you get depends on a tiny sidestep, and the prompt pill floats over the far ship (shots/046-t834.jpg). Suggestion: put each boarding point at the pier edge beside its own ship (e.g. z = ±2.2) so walking toward a ship gives its prompt.
- **[polish] [feel]** Six take/replace round trips with identical animation. Only swaps 1, 3 and 6 have a line, so trips 4–5 are ~20 s of pure fetching on a second playthrough. Suggestion: on replay (`voice.said` for `half`), let the player carry two planks, or speed up the swap animation.

## Keep

- The trolley's replay text ("This time you chose the other way" / "The same choice again") correctly compares consecutive runs, including a pulled-then-put-back lever counting as "left alone".
- Journal typing is isolated: letters typed in the answer box (e, n, w, a, s, d) never move, interact or open the notebook. Answers survive Play again and show up editable in the desk journal.
- Spawns beside the portal you used (painting, book, both doors) work every time.
- The prompt for stepping off the vat's edge follows you round the whole rim (not a single hotspot).

## Harness notes
- `use Talk` picks the nearest interactable with that text even if disabled, and waits 20 s for it (Theseus keeper while in `work`). Worked around with `walk` + `press e`.
- `type` after `clicksel "#journal textarea"` inserted the text at the click point (mid-sentence), not at the end. Harmless, but the edited answer reads oddly.
